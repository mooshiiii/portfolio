Compliance reporting platform

Running · Paisley & District Scouts

Every volunteer in the district has training and checks to keep in date. This web app takes the exports from the Scouts' membership system and turns them into a compliance report for each of the district's 12 groups and units, plus a district summary. Each volunteer gets one email listing exactly what they still need to do, even if they hold several roles.

The district now approves new appointments only when everyone involved is compliant, and these reports are how that's checked.

How I designed the HQ network

The district headquarters needed reliable Wi-Fi, CCTV and controlled door access on a volunteer budget, and it had to be something volunteers could look after once I'd built it.

Decisions

  1. One platform. Cameras, doors, sensors and Wi-Fi all run on UniFi, so a volunteer manages the whole building from one app instead of four.
  2. Rack, not wall unit. I first specified an all-in-one UniFi Dream Wall. Its total PoE budget isn't clearly published, so I moved to a UDM-Pro with a separate 24-port PoE switch and a patch panel, which also leaves room to grow.
  3. Power budget before buying. I added up the draw of every camera, reader, lock and the siren before choosing the switch, rather than finding out on the day.
  4. Cable once. Cat6A shielded, low-smoke cable carries 10 Gbps over a full 100 m run, so the building shouldn't need recabling for a decade. I'm using pass-through connectors so I can check each wire's order before crimping, and I'll test every run.

The equipment is on order. I'll add installation photos and what I learned once it's in.
