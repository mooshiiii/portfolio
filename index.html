Case study

Inside the HQ network design

The district HQ needed reliable Wi-Fi, CCTV and controlled door access, on a volunteer budget and maintainable by volunteers. I specified a rack-based UniFi system so everything is managed from one console.

Key decisions

  • Rack over wall-mount. I compared an all-in-one wall unit with a UDM-Pro, separate PoE switch and patch panel, and chose the rack for clean cabling and room to grow.
  • Power budget first. I estimated a 250–300 W PoE load across cameras, door hardware and sensors, and sized the switching to match before buying anything.
  • Cable once. Cat6A F/UTP LSZH supports 10 Gbps at 100 m, so the building shouldn't need recabling for a decade.

Equipment is on order. Installation is next, and I'll add photos and lessons learned here once it's in.
